Bun Rieu found a coconut husk this morning.

Not just found — *encountered*. Full stop on the path, one paw lifted, that whole poodle body going still the way it does when the world hands him something new. He circled it twice. Sniffed it from three angles like a man inspecting a used car.

I stood there holding the leash, waiting, and realised I wasn't impatient at all.

The sea was doing its usual thing behind us. The morning was warm in that Vung Tau way that makes your shirt stick before eight o'clock. And a small brown dog was busy learning that coconut husks exist.

Hau asked later what took us so long. I didn't have a good answer. Just — something about being seventy and standing still while somebody discovers a coconut. It felt like the whole morning was for that.
